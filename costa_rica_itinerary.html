💡 Practical Tips

💵 Currency: 500 CRC ≈ $1 USD. Both accepted. Cash for sodas & parking.
🚗 Car: 4x4 recommended for mountains. $50-70/day. Paved to Tamarindo.
🌧️ Weather: Sept = rainy season. Afternoon showers, lush green, fewer crowds.
🥘 Food: Sodas (local diners) = $5-8 casados. Mid-range = $12-20.
🔒 Safety: Don't leave valuables on Tamarindo beaches. Nothing visible in parked cars.
